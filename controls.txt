===============================================================================
  POSSESSED  //  CONTROLS QUICK REFERENCE
  Castle Danger Music Company  //  Pure Vocal Destruction
===============================================================================

Possessed is an insert effect. Put it on a vocal track (or any track: guitar,
synth, drums) like you would an EQ or a reverb.

Controls are listed top to bottom, left to right, the way they're laid out on
the plugin.

Tip: double-click a value readout under a knob to type an exact number
(e.g. "-12 st", "350 ms", "2.5 kHz", "40%").

Latency: Possessed delays the audio by 2048 samples (about 43 ms at 48 kHz)
and reports it to your DAW, which compensates automatically. You'll hear the
delay only if you monitor a live microphone through it.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HEADER
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  PRESET MENU (top right)
      17 factory sounds, plus your own saved presets under USER PRESETS.
      The < and > arrows step through both lists.

      Init (Clean)        Demon               Lizzy's Playhouse   The Haunting
      Possessed           Legion              Hellhound           The Entity
      Static From Beyond  Old Witch           Siren of the Dead   Ventriloquist Dummy
      Behind You          The Drowned         Found Footage       Cult Chant
      Wendigo

      Factory presets keep your INPUT knob and leave the FX RACK alone, so a
      stutter pattern you've programmed survives preset browsing.

  SAVE PRESET...
      Saves every setting (including the FX rack) under a name you type.
      Saving with an existing name asks before replacing it.

  DELETE
      Deletes the selected user preset (asks first). Greyed out for factory
      presets.

      User presets are .possessed files you can back up or share:
        macOS:   ~/Library/Application Support/Castle Danger/Possessed/Presets/
        Windows: %APPDATA%\Castle Danger\Possessed\Presets\

  THE EYES
      Glow brighter and widen with your signal level. Just for fun (and fear).


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VOICE  (the main transformed voice)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  PITCH      -24 to +24 semitones. Down = bigger and darker, up = smaller.
  FORMANT    -12 to +12 semitones. Moves the "size" of the throat without
             changing the note. Down = huge monster, up = small child or doll.
             Pitch and formant together decide the character.
  WHISPER    Turns the voice into a breathy, pitchless whisper.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LAYER  (a second voice from the same performance)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  PITCH / FORMANT   Same as above, for the second voice.
  LEVEL             How loud the layer is. Try an octave below for a demon
                    under you, or an octave above for a ghost.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DEMON
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  GROWL      Adds sub-octaves: one octave down, then two as you turn it up.
  RASP       Creaky vocal-fry rattle and roughness.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RING MOD / DISTORT / FILTER / WOBBLE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  RING MOD     MIX and FREQ (5 Hz - 2 kHz). Low = stuttery and robotic,
               high = metallic and inhuman.
  DISTORT      Type menu: Tube, Fuzz, Bitcrush, Radio (band-limited and
               crackling), Saturate (warm grit that keeps words clear).
               DRIVE sets the amount (0 = off).
  FILTER       Type menu: Off, Low-pass, Band-pass, High-pass.
               CUTOFF and RESO (resonance).
  WOBBLE       WARBLE: seasick tape-style pitch wobble.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IN / OUT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  INPUT      Gain into the plugin (affects how hard distortion is hit).
  DRY/WET    Blend of your untouched voice and the processed one.
  OUTPUT     Final level. Meters show input and output.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LEGION / REVERSE / DELAY / REVERB / LO-FI
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  LEGION     VOICES: a crowd of drifting, detuned copies ("we are many").
             SPREAD: how far apart they drift.
  REVERSE    MIX and LENGTH: backwards echoes that swell into each word.
  DELAY      Type menu: Digital, Ping-Pong, Analog (darker, gritty repeats),
             Tape (wobbling, warm, degrading). MIX, TIME, FEEDBACK.
  REVERB     MIX and SIZE (tails up to about 14 seconds).
  LO-FI      Global, applied to the whole output.
             LO-FI: narrows the sound, adds wobble and saturation, then
             crushes bits and sample rate as you turn it up.
             NOISE: tape hiss and crackle.


===============================================================================
FX RACK  (bottom row)
===============================================================================
Each effect has a glowing ON switch. The switches are normal plugin
parameters: automate them in your DAW to hit an effect on an exact word or
beat, or map them to a controller with your DAW's MIDI learn. A section
lights up while it's engaged.

  STUTTER
      A 16-step sequencer locked to your song's tempo. LIT STEPS CUT THE
      VOLUME. Click steps to light them. The current step glows as it plays.
      RATE (menu)   Step size: 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32, 1/8T, 1/16T.
      STEPS         Pattern length, 1-16 (steps past it are dimmed).
      DEPTH         How far the volume drops on a lit step.
      SMOOTH        Edge softness: low = hard chop, high = pulsing.
      TAILS         On: delay and reverb ring through the cuts.
                    Off: everything is chopped.
      The cuts land exactly on your DAW's beats. With the DAW stopped, the
      pattern free-runs at the session tempo.

  REPEAT
      Grabs a tempo-synced slice the moment you switch it on and loops it
      ("s-s-s-stutter").
      LENGTH (menu)  1/4 to 1/64.
      DROP           Pitch change per repeat. Negative = sinking and dying,
                     positive = rising panic.

  TAPE STOP
      Everything slows and drops in pitch until it grinds to silence.
      TIME   How long it takes to stop. Switch off to snap back.

  FREEZE
      Holds the last moment as a drifting, slightly detuned drone (a frozen
      scream) under the vocal.
      SIZE   Grain length: short = buzzy, long = smeared.
      MIX    How loud the frozen sound is.

  DIVE
      Slides the transformed voice down (or up).
      AMOUNT   -24 to +24 semitones.
      TIME     How long the slide takes.
